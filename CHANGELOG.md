# Beta v1.0
**Release Date:** July 31, 2026

## Release Summary

This is the first public beta release of SafeChat AI SOC. It includes the complete Security Operations Center platform with AI-assisted threat detection, incident management, executive reporting, customer dashboards, threat hunting, analytics, and Docker-based deployment.
